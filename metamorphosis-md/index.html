Frequently asked questions

Answers to what doctors usually ask.

Do I need internet to use it?

No. Metamorphosis MD is 100% offline. Internet is only needed for the optional email appointment sync feature (which uses YOUR Gmail, not our servers).

Is my patients' data safe?

Yes. All data is stored locally on your computer (SQLite database + uploads folder). Nothing is ever sent to our servers or any third party. You are the Data Controller under GDPR.

Do I need a credit card to start the trial?

No. The 14-day trial requires no card. Try every feature, then decide.

What happens after my trial ends?

You decide. If you love Metamorphosis MD, buy a lifetime license for €49 — one payment, yours forever. If not, just uninstall. No charges.

Can I use one license on multiple PCs?

One license works on one PC. The license is bound to your computer's hardware ID (HWID). If you change computers, contact support and we'll issue a new key.

Does it work on Mac or Linux?

No, Metamorphosis MD is Windows-only (Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit).

Is Metamorphosis MD a medical device?

No. Metamorphosis MD is not a medical device under MDR (EU 2017/745). It is a recording and data management tool. The doctor bears sole responsibility for clinical decisions. This is stated explicitly in the EULA.
