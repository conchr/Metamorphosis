Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2025

1. Overview

Metamorphosis ("we", "us") develops Windows desktop applications. This policy explains how our apps and website handle your information.

2. Our Apps Are Offline

All our software (CopyCat, LangIndicator, and future apps) operates entirely offline. We do not collect, transmit, or store:

  • Your content (snippets, text, configurations)
  • Your browsing activity
  • Your personal files
  • Any telemetry or analytics

3. Data Stored Locally

Our apps store the following only on your computer and never transmit it:

  • Configuration preferences
  • User content (e.g. snippets)
  • License key
  • Hardware identifier (HWID) — used only to validate your license

4. Website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages and does not use cookies, analytics, or tracking. GitHub may log visitor IP addresses for security and operational purposes. See GitHub's Privacy Statement.

5. Purchases

When you buy a license, payment is processed by Lemon Squeezy (our Merchant of Record). They collect your name, email, and billing information. See Lemon Squeezy's Privacy Policy.

6. Support

If you contact us at metamorphosisMD@protonmail.com, we keep your email only as long as needed to resolve your request.

7. Your Rights (GDPR)

EU residents have the right to access, correct, or delete personal data. Contact us at metamorphosisMD@protonmail.com.

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page.
